# Springfield City (Robertson County, TN) Courts
## Springfield Municipal Court
- Serves: Springfield City
- Location: City Hall, 405 N Main Street, Springfield, TN
- Court Dates: 1st and 3rd Monday of each month at 3:30 PM
- City Judge: Gary Dilliha (Interim: Charlotte Fleming)
- Court Clerk: Contact via Police Records Division at (615) 384-8422
- Court Costs: $115 (may be waived)
- Payments: Accepted at Police Records Division, 802 Willow Street
- Website: https://springfieldtn.gov/city-court
- Jurisdiction: City ordinance violations
- Tennessee State Courts: https://www.tncourts.gov
- Note: Municipal City Court; located in City Hall; meets twice monthly; payments at Police Records
- Handles: City ordinance violations, traffic infractions
