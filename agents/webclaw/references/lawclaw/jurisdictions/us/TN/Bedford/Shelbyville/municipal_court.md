# Shelbyville City (Bedford County, TN) Courts
## Shelbyville Municipal Court
- Serves: Shelbyville City
- Location: Shelbyville Recreation Center, 220 Tulip Tree Road, Shelbyville, TN
- Court Date: First Monday of each month at 1:00 PM
- Judge: John T. Bobo
- City Attorney: Ginger Shofner
- City Court Clerk: Amber Chapmen
- Citation Payments: Must be paid by noon on court date
- Pay by phone: 1-877-618-7898 (use citation number as account number)
- Note: Failure to appear or pay may result in driver's license suspension
- Website: https://www.shelbyvilletn.org
- Jurisdiction: City ordinance violations
- Tennessee State Courts: https://www.tncourts.gov
- Note: Municipal City Court; held at Recreation Center (not City Hall); meets once monthly
- Handles: City ordinance violations, traffic infractions
