- https://www.cityofsitka.com
- City Hall: 100 Lincoln Street, Sitka, AK 99835
- Phone: (907) 747-1800
- Mayor: Steven Eisenbeisz
- Government Type: Unified Home Rule City-Borough
- Population: ~8,500
- Coordinates: 57.0531° N, 135.3300° W
- Note: On Baranof Island in the Inside Passage of Southeast Alaska; historic Russian capital of Alaska (1799-1867); known for the transfer ceremony site where Russia formally handed Alaska to the U.S. (1867); home to Sitka National Historical Park (totem poles), St. Michael's Russian Orthodox Cathedral, the Alaska Raptor Center, and the Sheet'ká Kwáan Naa Kahídi (Tlingit community house); accessible only by air (Sitka Airport - SIT) or sea (Alaska Marine Highway)
