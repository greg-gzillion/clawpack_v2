- Note: On Kodiak Island in the Gulf of Alaska; largest community on Kodiak Island and home to the largest U.S. Coast Guard base (Base Kodiak); known for world-class salmon and halibut fishing, Kodiak brown bears, and the Kodiak National Wildlife Refuge; accessible only by air (Kodiak Airport - ADQ) or sea (Alaska Marine Highway); originally a Russian fur trading settlement
