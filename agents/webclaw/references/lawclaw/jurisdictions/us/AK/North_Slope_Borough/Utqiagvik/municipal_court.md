- North Slope Borough | Population: ~4,900
- Coordinates: 71.2906° N, 156.7886° W
- Note: Northernmost city in the United States (formerly known as Barrow, name changed in 2016); located on the Arctic Ocean coast above the Arctic Circle; accessible only by air (Wiley Post–Will Rogers Memorial Airport - BRW); predominantly Iñupiat Eskimo community; known for the midnight sun (85 days of continuous daylight) and polar night (65 days of darkness); center of Iñupiat culture and subsistence whaling; home to Ilisagvik College (tribal college) and the Inupiat Heritage Center
