- https://www.petersburgak.gov

- Phone: (907) 772-4425
- Borough Manager: Steve Giesbrecht
- Government Type: Unified Home Rule Borough (City-Borough)
- Population: ~3,000
- Coordinates: 56.8129° N, 132.9555° W
- Note: On Mitkof Island in the Inside Passage of Southeast Alaska; known as "Little Norway" for its strong Norwegian heritage; founded by Norwegian fisherman Peter Buschmann in the late 1890s; economy based on commercial fishing (halibut, salmon, crab) and seafood processing; accessible only by air (Petersburg Airport - PSG) or sea (Alaska Marine Highway); home to the annual Little Norway Festival
