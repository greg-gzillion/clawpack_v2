# Juneau City and Borough (Alaska) Courts

## Juneau Municipal Court — NONE
- Status: **UNIFIED MUNICIPALITY — NO STANDALONE MUNICIPAL COURT**
- Juneau is a unified home-rule city-borough and Alaska's state capital
- All judicial matters handled by: **State of Alaska Court System**
- Juneau Superior Court & District Court: Diamond Court Building, 123 4th Street, Juneau, AK 99801
- Phone: (907) 463-4700
- Superior Court: Handles major civil, criminal, family, probate cases
- District Court: Handles misdemeanors, small claims, preliminary felony hearings
- Court Website: https://courts.alaska.gov/courtdir/1ju.htm
- Alaska Court Records: https://alaskacourtrecords.us

- Population: ~32,000

- Note: Only state capital in the U.S. inaccessible by road; located on the Gastineau Channel in the Alaska Panhandle; accessible only by air (Juneau International Airport - JNU) or sea (Alaska Marine Highway ferry); known for Mendenhall Glacier, Tracy Arm Fjord, whale watching, and the Alaska State Capitol

- Permit Center Phone: (907) 586-0770
- Website: https://www.juneau.org/community-development
