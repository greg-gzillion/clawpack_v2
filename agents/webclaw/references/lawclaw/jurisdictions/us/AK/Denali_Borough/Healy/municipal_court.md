## Building Permits
- Issued by: Denali Borough Planning & Zoning Department
- Contact: (907) 683-1330 | planner@denaliborough.org
- Website: https://www.denaliborough.org/planning-zoning
- **Permit Types:**
  - Land Use Permit: Required for any construction or land development
  - Building Permit: Needed for structures over 200 sq ft or structural modifications
  - Zoning Approval: Must be obtained before construction begins
- **Process:** Contact Planning Dept → Submit plans and application form → Pay fees → Review (5–10 business days) → Schedule inspections
- Denali Borough Code: https://codepublishing.com (search Denali Borough)
- **NOTE:** Denali Borough has not adopted a building code outside city limits (only City of Anderson requires permits within borough)
