# Healy Municipal Court

- Alaska Court System (Denali Borough served by Fairbanks District Court)
- Court Website: https://courts.alaska.gov
## Borough Website
- https://denaliborough.org
## Police Department
- Alaska State Troopers — Healy Post
- Address: PO Box 28, Cantwell, AK 99729 (serves Healy area)
- Phone: (907) 683-2232
- Website: https://dps.alaska.gov/ast
## Detention Center
- No local jail. Detainees held at Fairbanks Correctional Center or Anchorage Correctional Complex.
## Hunting & Fishing
- Alaska Department of Fish and Game — https://adfg.alaska.gov
- Hunting: moose, bear, small game
- Fishing: salmon, trout
- Licenses available online
## Library
- No dedicated public library in Healy. Residents access services through mobile or regional libraries in Fairbanks.
