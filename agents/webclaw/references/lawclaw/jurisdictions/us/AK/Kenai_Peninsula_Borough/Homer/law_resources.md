## Hunting & Fishing Licenses
- Alaska Department of Fish and Game — https://adfg.alaska.gov
- Purchase online or at local vendors (e.g., The Tackle Shack on Homer Spit)
- Non-resident: 1-day, 3-day, 7-day, 14-day, or annual
- King Salmon Stamp required for king salmon
- Digital licenses allowed on mobile devices
- Hunting Regulations: https://adfg.alaska.gov
