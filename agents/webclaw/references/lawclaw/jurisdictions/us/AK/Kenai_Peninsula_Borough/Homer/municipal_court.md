## Detention Center
- Homer Community Jail
- Address: 625 Grubstake Avenue, Homer, AK 99603 (co-located with police)
- Phone: (907) 235-3150

## Hunting & Fishing Licenses
- Alaska Department of Fish and Game
- Purchase online at adfg.alaska.gov or in person at local vendors (e.g., The Tackle Shack on the Homer Spit)
- Non-resident Licenses: 1-day, 3-day, 7-day, 14-day, or annual
- King Salmon Stamp required for king salmon
- Digital licenses allowed on mobile devices
