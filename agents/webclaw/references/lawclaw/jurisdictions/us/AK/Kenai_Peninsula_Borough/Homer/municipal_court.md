## Building Permits
- Issued by: City of Homer Planning Department
- Phone: (907) 235-3106 | Email: planning@cityofhomer-ak.gov
- Online Portal: Customer Portal (apply and track projects online)
- Website: https://www.cityofhomer-ak.gov/planning/permits
- **Key Requirements:**
  - Zoning Permit: Required prior to clearing land or new construction (including additions)
  - Flood Hazard Areas: Require a Flood Development Permit
  - Wetlands: Require US Army Corps of Engineers permit
- **Exemptions:** Detached accessory buildings under 200 sq ft may be exempt but must meet setbacks
- **Review Time:** Up to 10 working days for complete submittals
- **Fees:** Check the Fees Webpage for current rates
- **IMPORTANT:** The City of Homer does NOT issue building permits or conduct building inspections; handled by State of Alaska or private inspectors
