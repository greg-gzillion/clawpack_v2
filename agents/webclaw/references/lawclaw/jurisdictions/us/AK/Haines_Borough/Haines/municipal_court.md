## Building Permits
- Issued by: Haines Borough Planning & Zoning Department
- Planner: Chen Wu, Planner II | (907) 766-6410 | planner@haines.ak.us
- Planning & Zoning Tech: Donna Lambert | (907) 766-6401
- Deputy Clerk: Kiersten Long | (907) 766-6412
- Admin Assistant: Margarette Jones | (907) 766-6400
- Office: Borough Administration Building, 103 Third Avenue S., P.O. Box 1209, Haines, AK 99827
- Hours: Mon–Fri 9 AM – 5 PM
- Website: https://www.hainesalaska.gov/planningzoning
- **Permit Types:**
  - Land Use Permit: Required for new construction or footprint changes within townsite
  - Construction Declaration: Mandatory for projects >$5,000 value or >500 sq ft outside townsite
  - Site Development Permit: Needed for grading/filling >1,000 cubic yards
  - Conditional Use Permit (CUP): Required for high-impact uses (heliports, commercial events) — $150 fee
  - Sign Permit Application | Variance Application | Water-Sewer NEW Service App
- **Process:** Determine zoning via Parcel Viewer → Contact Planner → Submit application → Attend public hearing if required → Receive permit with conditions
- Zoning Use Chart: https://hainesborough.borough.codes/HBC/18.70.040
