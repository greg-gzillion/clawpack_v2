- City Hall: 434 Max Italio Drive, Yakutat, AK 99689
- Phone: (907) 784-3323
- Borough Manager: Tina Ryman
- Borough Clerk: Ricardo Tejeda
- Government Type: Unified Home Rule City-Borough
- Population: ~600
- Coordinates: 59.5514° N, 139.7369° W
- Note: On the Gulf of Alaska at Yakutat Bay; one of the most isolated communities in Alaska accessible only by air (Yakutat Airport - YAK) or sea; world-famous for surfing (Yakutat Surfing) on the Gulf of Alaska; known for the Hubbard Glacier, Wrangell-St. Elias National Park, and the Yakutat Tlingit Tribe; legendary steelhead and salmon fishing on the Situk River; surrounded by the Tongass National Forest
