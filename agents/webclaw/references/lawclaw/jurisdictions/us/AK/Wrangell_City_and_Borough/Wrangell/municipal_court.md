- Population: ~2,100
- Coordinates: 56.4708° N, 132.3767° W
- Note: On Wrangell Island in the Inside Passage of Southeast Alaska; one of the oldest non-Native settlements in Alaska (established 1834 by Russian fur traders); governed under Tlingit law prior to Russian arrival; known for the Stikine River, Chief Shakes Tribal House, Wrangell Garnet Ledge, and Anan Bear and Wildlife Observatory; accessible only by air (Wrangell Airport - WRG) or sea (Alaska Marine Highway)
