## Building Permits
- Issued by: City of Fairbanks Building Department
- Address: City Hall, 800 Cushman Street, Fairbanks, AK 99701
- Phone: (907) 459-6720 | Fax: (907) 459-6719
- Hours: Mon–Fri 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM
- Website: https://www.fairbanks.gov/building
- Permit required: Structures over 200 sq ft, structural modifications, Sheetrock removal, new/changed uses or occupancy
- Zoning Permit from FNSB Planning Department required before applying
- Plan Review: 6–10 business days; corrections communicated in writing
- Inspections: Call (907) 459-6720 at least 24 hours in advance (foundation, framing, final, etc.)
- For projects without plans: download, complete, and FAX application to (907) 459-6719
- For larger projects: attach two full sets of plans (PDF for commercial)
- **NOTE:** Building permits only required within City of Fairbanks and City of North Pole; FNSB at-large has no adopted building code
- FNSB Permitting & Inspections: https://co.fairbanks.ak.us
- Permit Applications: https://fairbanks.gov (Permit Applications *UPDATED*)
