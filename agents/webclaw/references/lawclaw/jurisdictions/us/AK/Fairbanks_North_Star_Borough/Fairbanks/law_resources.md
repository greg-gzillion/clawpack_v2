## Building Permits
- Building Dept: City Hall, 800 Cushman Street | (907) 459-6720 | Fax: (907) 459-6719
- Hours: Mon–Fri 8 AM – 4 PM | Website: https://www.fairbanks.gov/building

- Plan review: 6–10 business days | Inspections: call 24 hrs ahead
