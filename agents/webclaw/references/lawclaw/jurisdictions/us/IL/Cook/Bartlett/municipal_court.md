# Bartlett Municipal Court
## Court Information
- Bartlett does not have a municipal court
- Parking and ordinance violations handled through administrative hearings
- Criminal and traffic cases heard in Cook County Circuit Court – Third Municipal District (Rolling Meadows)
- Court Website: https://www.cookcountycourts.gov
