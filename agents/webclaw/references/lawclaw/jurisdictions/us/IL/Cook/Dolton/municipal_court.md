# Dolton Municipal Court
## Court Information
- Dolton does not have a standalone municipal court
- Traffic and housing violations handled through administrative hearings
- Legal matters processed under Cook County Circuit Court – Sixth Municipal District (Markham)
- Court Website: https://www.cookcountycourts.gov
