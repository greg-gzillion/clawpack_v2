## Building Permits
- Zoning Officer: Michael Rhoads
- Address: 225 Market Street, Sunbury, PA 17801
- Phone: (570) 286-4207
- Code Administration Office: Administers Uniform Construction Code (Act 45)
- Process:
  1. Submit completed application with plans and fees to Zoning Officer
  2. Review for Zoning Ordinance compliance
  3. Zoning Permit Placard issued (typically 5–7 business days)
  4. UCC Building Permit forwarded to third-party agency if required
  5. Both permits issued together when applicable
- Required Forms: Building Permit Application, Driveway Permit Application, Subdivision & Land Development Plans, Stormwater Management Worksheet
- Zoning Ordinance & Forms: https://ecode360.com/11631511
- Building Code Administration: https://ecode360.com/11628862
- Permits Required: Construction, sheds, plumbing, electrical, zoning, driveway, stormwater management
