## Building Permits
- Office: Borough of Lansford (PA Uniform Construction Code - UCC)
- Steps:
  1. Determine Need: Permits for new construction, additions, major renovations, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, pools
  2. Obtain Approvals: Check zoning and HARB (Historic Architectural Review Board) if applicable
  3. Submit Application: UCC-3 Application; 3 sets construction drawings (18"x24" min, to scale); seal if structural/egress changes; 4 site plan copies
  4. Fee: Payable to Commonwealth of Pennsylvania (varies by project)
  5. Review Time: Up to 30 days; expedited review available for additional fee
  6. Inspections: Required at key stages (footings, framing, final)
  7. Certificate of Occupancy: Issued after final inspection
- Submit To: 1 West Ridge Street, Lansford, PA 18232 — (570) 645-3900
- PA UCC Portal: Apply for UCC Building Permit (pa.gov/dli — note: may return 404)
- Permits Required: New construction, additions, renovations, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, pools, HARB review
