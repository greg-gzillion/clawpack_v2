## Building Permits
- Office: Borough of Nesquehoning (PA Uniform Construction Code - UCC)
- Determine Need: Required for decks >30" high, sheds, pools ≥24" deep, renovations, electrical, plumbing
- Submit: UCC-3 Form; site plan and construction drawings (4 copies); fee to Commonwealth of PA
- Submit To: 114 W. Catawissa St, Nesquehoning, PA 18240
- Online Forms: https://nesquehoning.org/forms-permits
- Inspections Required: Footing, framing, final
- Zoning Officer/Building Inspector: Via Borough Office
- Permits Required: Decks (>30"), sheds, pools (≥24"), renovations, electrical, plumbing, UCC
