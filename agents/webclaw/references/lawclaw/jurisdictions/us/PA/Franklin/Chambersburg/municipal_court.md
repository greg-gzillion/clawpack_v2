## Building Permits
- Land Use and Community Development Department — (717) 251-2417
- Contact: Jamie Foose, jfoose@chambersburgpa.gov
- Address: Borough Hall, 100 South Second Street, Second Floor, Chambersburg, PA 17201
- Website: https://www.chambersburgpa.gov/resident/permits.html
- Process: Land Use Permit required before Building Permit; validity 1 year (construction within 6 months)
- Fees: Land Use/Use & Occupancy (no fee); Excavation $45 non-state / $75 state route; Curb/Sidewalk $45/$75; Zoning Variance $250; Rental Inspection $75 every 3 years
- Note: Plumbing/electrical permits require coordination with PA Municipal Code Alliance (PMCA)
- Permits Required: New construction, additions, decks, roofing, electrical, plumbing, HVAC
- Inspections: Required at multiple stages
