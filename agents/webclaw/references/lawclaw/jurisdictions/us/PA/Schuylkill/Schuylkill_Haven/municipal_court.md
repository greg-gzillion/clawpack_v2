- Serves: Schuylkill Haven Borough

- Nearest MDJ: Magisterial District Court 21-3-04
- Address: 333 Centre Avenue, Schuylkill Haven, PA 17972
- Phone: (570) 385-2841
- Website: https://www.schuylkillcountypa.gov/courts
- Handles: Traffic, criminal, and civil cases
