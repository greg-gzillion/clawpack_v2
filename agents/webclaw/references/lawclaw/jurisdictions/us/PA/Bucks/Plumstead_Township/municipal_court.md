## Building Permits
- Zoning & Building Department — (215) 766-8914
- Contact: Michelle Storti, Municipal Administrator / RTK Admin — mstorti@plumstead.gov
- Address: 5186 Stump Road, Pipersville, PA 18947
- Building Permit Guide: PDF Download available at plumstead.org
- Key Requirements:
  - Submit 2 paper copies + 1 digital PDF of plans
  - Commercial projects require architect/engineer seals
  - Zoning and stormwater approvals required before building permit review
  - Contractors must provide proof of Workers' Comp and PA Home Improvement Contractor registration
  - Electrical inspections must be done by approved third-party agencies
  - Final inspection requires submission of electrical "cut card"
- Inspection Policy: Schedule 24 hours in advance with Phil Neas at (215) 766-8914
- Required Inspections: Footing, foundation, framing, insulation, final
- Authorized Electrical Agencies:
  - Code Inspections, Inc. — (215) 672-9400
  - Middle Atlantic Electrical Inspections — (215) 322-2626
  - United Inspection Agency — (215) 542-9977
  - Grill 1 Inspections — (215) 694-6525
- Website: https://www.plumstead.org (Building Permit Procedures)
- Permits Required: New construction, additions, decks, roofing, electrical, plumbing, HVAC
