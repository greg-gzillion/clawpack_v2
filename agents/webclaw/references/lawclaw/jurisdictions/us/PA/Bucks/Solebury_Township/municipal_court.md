## Building Permits
- Planning, Code Enforcement & Permits Department — (215) 297-5656
- Zoning Officer: Zachary Zubris — zzubris@soleburytwp.org
- Address: 3092 Sugan Road, Solebury, PA 18963
- Permit Types: Building, grading, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, zoning
- Processing Time: Up to 45 days (30 days zoning review + 15 days building inspection)
- Grading Permit: Required for earth disturbance >2,000 sq ft (must precede building permit)
- Use & Occupancy Permit: Required before occupancy (per Ordinance §2105)
- Forms & Docs: Planning & Zoning Page available
- Website: https://www.soleburytwp.org (Planning, Code Enforcement & Permits)
- Permits Required: New construction, additions, decks, roofing, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, grading
