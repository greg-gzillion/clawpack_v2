## Building Permits
- Building & Zoning Department — (724) 443-5921
- Contact: Lyndsey Smith, Building & Zoning Clerk — lsmith@richland.pa.us
- Permit Portal: Apply Online via township website
- Requirements:
  - Permits required for new construction, additions, decks >30", sheds ≥1,000 sq ft, pools, electrical, and more
  - Submit two paper copies and one digital PDF
  - Zoning certification required
  - Inspections: footing, foundation, framing, insulation, final
  - Electrical inspections must be done by approved third-party agencies (e.g., Code Inspections, Inc., United Inspection Agency)
- Forms: Building & Zoning Forms available
- Website: https://www.richlandtownship.org
- Permits Required: New construction, additions, decks, roofing, electrical, plumbing, HVAC
