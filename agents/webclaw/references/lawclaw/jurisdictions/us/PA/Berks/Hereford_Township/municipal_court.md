## Building Permits
- Managed by Zoning Officer and Building Code Official (both Kraft Municipal Group)
- Required Permits:
  - New construction, additions, decks
  - Sheds (>150 sq ft requires building permit)
  - Driveways, fences, pools
  - Re-roofing (if changing structure)
  - Electrical, plumbing, HVAC, and alternative energy systems
- Zoning Officer: Kraft Municipal Group — (610) 777-1311
