## Building Permits
- Managed by Community Development Department
- Required Permits:
  - New construction, additions, decks
  - Sheds ≥200 sq ft
  - Driveways, pools
  - Re-roofing (>25% of roof)
  - Electrical, plumbing, HVAC, signage
- Fee Schedule:
  - Application Fee: $35
  - Residential New Construction: $595 (up to 3,500 sq ft), $8.50 per 100 sq ft over
  - Decks/Outbuildings: $120 (up to 500 sq ft)
- Contact: (610) 683-3290
- Address: 324 West Main Street, Kutztown, PA 19530
- Forms: https://www.kutztownboro.org/departments/communitydevelopment/permits/
- Website: https://www.kutztownboro.org
- Permits Required: New construction, additions, decks, sheds (≥200 sq ft), driveways, pools, re-roofing (>25%), electrical, plumbing, HVAC, signage
