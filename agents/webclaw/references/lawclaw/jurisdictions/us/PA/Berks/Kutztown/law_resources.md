## Building Permits
- Community Development Dept: (610) 683-3290 | 324 West Main Street, Kutztown, PA 19530
- Fee Schedule: Application $35 | New Construction $595 (≤3,500 sq ft, $8.50/100 sq ft over) | Decks/Outbuildings $120 (≤500 sq ft)
- Required: New construction, additions, decks, sheds ≥200 sq ft, driveways, pools, re-roofing (>25%), electrical, plumbing, HVAC, signage
