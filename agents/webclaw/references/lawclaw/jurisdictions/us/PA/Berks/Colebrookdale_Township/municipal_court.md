## Building Permits
- Building and zoning permits managed through township government
- Key Steps:
  1. Application: Submit a preliminary plan to the Planning Commission
  2. Review: Board of Commissioners has 90 days to act
  3. Final Plan: Must be submitted within 12 months of preliminary approval
  4. Inspections: Required during and after construction
  5. Certificate of Occupancy: Issued upon final inspection
- Required Documents: Site plan, Zoning permit application, Building plans, Erosion and sediment control plan (if applicable)
- Building Code Official: Contact via township office
- Zoning Officer: Available for pre-application consultations
- Website: https://colebrookdale.org
- External Resource: LicenseSuite — https://www.businesslicenses.com
- Permits Required: New construction, additions, decks, roofing, electrical, plumbing, HVAC
