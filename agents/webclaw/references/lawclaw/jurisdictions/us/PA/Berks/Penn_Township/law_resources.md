## Building Permits
- Zoning Officer / Building Inspector: Marty Sowers, Light-Heigel & Assoc. — (800) 257-2190
- Two-tier shed permits: >99 sq ft = zoning permit; >1,000 sq ft = building permit
- Required: New construction, additions, decks, sheds, driveways, pools, re-roofing, electrical, plumbing
- Forms & Info: https://penntownshipberks.org
