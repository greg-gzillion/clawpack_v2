## Building Permits
- Managed by Township Building & Zoning Department
- Key Steps:
  1. Application: Submit to township office or electronically
  2. Review: By Zoning Officer and third-party agency
  3. Inspections: Conducted by Kraft Municipal Group
  4. Certificate of Occupancy: Issued after final inspection
- Required Forms: Zoning Permit Application, Building Permit Application, Driveway Permit, Electrical Permit, Plumbing Permit, Re-Roof Permit (as needed)
- Zoning Officer: Andrew Duncan — (610) 367-6062 x1115 | Email: aduncan@douglasstownship.org
- Inspections: Kraft Municipal Group — (610) 775-7185
- Forms: https://www.douglasstownship.org/resources/documents-forms-permits/
- Website: https://www.douglasstownship.org
- Permits Required: New construction, additions, decks, roofing, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, driveways
