## Building Permits
- Administered by Zoning Officer (Keystone Consulting Engineers, 2025–present)
- Required Permits: Building, electrical, plumbing, mechanical permits, zoning permits, driveway permits, stormwater management plans, sign and utility permits
- Application Process:
  1. Obtain Forms: https://tildentownship.com/forms-permits
  2. Submit: In person (874 Hex Highway, Hamburg, PA), by mail, fax (610-562-5490), or email lramirez@tildentownship.com
  3. Review Period: Up to 30 days by Zoning Officer
  4. Fees: Posted annually (2026 Fee Schedule available online)
  5. Inspections: Scheduled through Building Inspection Underwriters (BIU) — Bill Murphy at (570) 499-7204
- Key Rules:
  - After-the-Fact Fee: $250 if construction begins before permit approval
  - Permit Expiry: One year from issuance; may be extended once for up to two additional years
- Address: 874 Hex Highway, Hamburg, PA 19526
- Website: https://tildentownship.com
- Permits Required: Building, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, zoning, driveway, stormwater management, sign, utility
