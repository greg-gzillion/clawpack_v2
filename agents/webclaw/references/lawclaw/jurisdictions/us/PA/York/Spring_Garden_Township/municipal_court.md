## Building Permits
- Code Administrators (Third-Party Agency) — (717) 859-3350
- Email: admin@codeadministrators.com
- Address: 340 Tri Hill Road, Suite A, York, PA 17403
- Fees: $35 minimum + $10 per $1,000 of construction cost after $3,000
- Website: https://www.springgardentwp.org/building-permits
- Required For: Construction, additions, decks, pools, sheds, impervious surfaces
- Inspections: Required at key phases; handled by third-party Code Administrators
- Permits Required: New construction, additions, decks, roofing, electrical, plumbing, HVAC
