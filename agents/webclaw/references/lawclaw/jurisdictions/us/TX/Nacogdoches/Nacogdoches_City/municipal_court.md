# Nacogdoches City (Nacogdoches County, TX) Courts
## Nacogdoches Municipal Court
- Serves: Nacogdoches City
- Location: 217 W Hospital Ave, Nacogdoches, TX 75961
- Phone: (936) 559-2540
- Payment Portal: https://tx-nacogdoches.civicplus.com/890/Payment-Information
- Website: https://tx-nacogdoches.civicplus.com/889/Municipal-Court
- Juvenile Policy: Under 17 must appear with parent/guardian; fines up to $500; DPS license suspension possible
- Expunction: Available on or after 17th birthday per Art. 45.0216
- Jurisdiction: City ordinance violations, traffic infractions

- Note: Municipal City Court; located in same building as Police Department
- Handles: City ordinance violations, traffic infractions, juvenile cases
