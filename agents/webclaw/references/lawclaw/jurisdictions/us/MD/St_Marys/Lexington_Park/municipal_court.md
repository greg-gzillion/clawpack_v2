# Lexington Park Courts
## Court Information
- Lexington Park is an unincorporated CDP without a municipal court
- Legal matters handled by the District Court and Circuit Court for St. Mary's County, located in Leonardtown
- District Court: https://www.mdcourts.gov/district/stm
- Circuit Court: https://www.mdcourts.gov/circuit/stm
