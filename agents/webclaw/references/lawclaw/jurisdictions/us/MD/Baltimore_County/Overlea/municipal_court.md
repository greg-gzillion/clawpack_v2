# Overlea Courts
## Court Information
- Overlea is an unincorporated community spanning Baltimore County and Baltimore City, without a municipal court
- Legal matters handled by Maryland District and Circuit Courts for Baltimore County and Baltimore City
- Baltimore County District Court: https://www.mdcourts.gov/district/baltco
- Baltimore City District Court: https://www.mdcourts.gov/district/bcity
- Circuit Court for Baltimore County: https://www.mdcourts.gov/circuit/baltco
- Circuit Court for Baltimore City: https://www.mdcourts.gov/circuit/bcity
