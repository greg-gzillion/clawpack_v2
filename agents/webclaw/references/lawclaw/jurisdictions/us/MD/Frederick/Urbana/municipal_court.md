# Urbana Courts
## Court Information
- Urbana is an unincorporated CDP without a municipal court
- Legal matters handled by the District Court and Circuit Court for Frederick County
- District Court of Maryland - Frederick: https://www.mdcourts.gov/district/directories/frederick
- Circuit Court for Frederick County: https://www.mdcourts.gov/circuit/frederick
