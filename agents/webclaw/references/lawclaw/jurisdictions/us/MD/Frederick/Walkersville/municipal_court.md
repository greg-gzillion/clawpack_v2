# Walkersville Courts
## Court Information
- Walkersville does not have a municipal court
- Legal matters handled by Maryland District and Circuit Courts for Frederick County
- District Court: https://www.mdcourts.gov/district/fred
- Circuit Court: https://www.mdcourts.gov/circuit/fred

## Detention Center
- Walkersville does not operate a holding facility
- Frederick County Detention Center — 5370 Public Safety Place, Frederick, MD 21704 — (301) 600-2200
- Inmate Roster: https://inmatesearch.fredericksheriff.org
## Building Permits
- Town Zoning Certificate: Required for most construction; issued by Town Planner
- Town Permit Info: https://www.walkersvillemd.gov/planning-commission/pages/permits
- Frederick County Building Permits: Required for structural work — https://www.frederickcountymd.gov/permits
