# Cumberland Courts
## Court Information
- Cumberland does not have a standalone municipal court
- Legal matters handled by the Circuit Court for Allegany County and the District Court of Maryland for Allegany County
- Circuit Court: https://www.mdcourts.gov/circuit/allegany
- District Court: https://www.mdcourts.gov/district/allegany
