# Annapolis Courts
## Court Information
- Annapolis does not have a municipal court
- Legal matters handled by the District Court of Maryland for Anne Arundel County and the Circuit Court for Anne Arundel County
- District Court: https://www.mdcourts.gov/district/arundel
- Circuit Court: https://www.mdcourts.gov/circuit/arundel
