# New Carrollton Courts
## Court Information
- New Carrollton does not have a municipal court
- Citations handled through Municipal Infractions; may be adjudicated in District Court of Maryland – Prince George's County
- Infraction Appeals: Request District Court trial or apply for Administrative Appeal Review (AAR)
- District Court: https://www.mdcourts.gov/district/pg
- Circuit Court for Prince George's County: https://www.mdcourts.gov/circuit/pg
