# College Park Courts
## Court Information
- College Park does not have a traditional municipal court
- Violations of city code (e.g., parking, housing) are issued as Municipal Infractions and adjudicated in District Court
- Infraction Appeals: Submit written request to publicservices@collegeparkmd.gov or mail to 7401 Baltimore Ave, Ste 201, College Park, MD 20740
- District Court of Maryland for Prince George's County: https://www.mdcourts.gov/district/pg
- Circuit Court for Prince George's County: https://www.mdcourts.gov/circuit/pg

## Police Department
- College Park does not have its own municipal police department
- Prince George's County Police Department (PGPD) — primary agency for 911 response, patrols, and investigations
- University of Maryland Police Department (UMPD) — 7569 Baltimore Ave, College Park, MD 20742 — Emergency: 301-405-3333 or #3333 — Non-Emergency: 301-405-3555 — Website: https://umpd.umd.edu
- Contract Police Program: 15.5 FTE off-duty PGPD officers funded by speed camera revenues
- PGPD Website: https://www.princegeorgescountymd.gov/police
## Detention Center
- College Park has no local jail
