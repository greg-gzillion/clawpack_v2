# College Park — Local Law Resources
## City Website
- https://www.collegeparkmd.gov — (240) 487-3500
- Address: 4500 Knox Road, College Park, MD 20740
- Email: publicinfo@collegeparkmd.gov
## Law Enforcement
- College Park does not have its own municipal police department
- Prince George's County Police Department (PGPD) — primary 911 response
- University of Maryland Police Department (UMPD) — 7569 Baltimore Ave — Emergency: 301-405-3333 | Non-Emergency: 301-405-3555 — https://umpd.umd.edu
- Contract Police Program: 15.5 FTE off-duty PGPD officers on city streets
- PGPD Website: https://www.princegeorgescountymd.gov/police
## Courts
- Municipal Infractions for city code violations; appeals via publicservices@collegeparkmd.gov or District Court
- District Court of Maryland for Prince George's County — https://www.mdcourts.gov/district/pg
- Circuit Court for Prince George's County — https://www.mdcourts.gov/circuit/pg
## Detention Center
- College Park has no local jail
- Prince George's County Correctional Center — 13400 Dille Drive, Upper Marlboro, MD 20772
- Inmate Locator: https://www.princegeorgescountymd.gov/corrections
