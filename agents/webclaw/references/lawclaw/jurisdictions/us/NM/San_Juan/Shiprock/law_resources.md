# Shiprock — Local Law Resources
## Community Website
- Shiprock is a census-designated place (CDP) within the Navajo Nation
- Governed by Shiprock Chapter of the Navajo Nation (12 elected members)
- Navajo Nation Official Site: https://www.navajo-nation.org
- Jurisdiction: Part of San Juan County, New Mexico
## Law Enforcement
- Shiprock Police Department — primary local law enforcement
- San Juan County Sheriff's Office — additional support
- Emergency: 911 | Non-Emergency (San Juan County): (505) 566-4500
## Courts
- Navajo Nation District Court – Shiprock: handles civil and criminal cases within Navajo Nation
- San Juan County Magistrate Court: 701 W. Broadway, Farmington, NM 87401 — (505) 566-4555
- San Juan County District Court: 871 South Butler Avenue, Farmington, NM 87401 — (505) 566-4545
  Website: https://www.nmcourts.gov
## Detention Center
- Shiprock Detention Center (New Facility — completed 2024) — Navajo Department of Corrections
  Website: https://opvp.navajo-nsn.gov
  Capacity: Up to 12 inmates (8 single cells, 1 communal cell)
  Note: Houses inmates locally, reducing long-distance transport to Kayenta, AZ
- San Juan County Adult Detention Center (Primary Jail) — 871 Andrea Drive, Farmington, NM 87401 — (505) 566-4500
  Website: https://www.sjcounty.net/adult-detention-center
## Building Permits
- San Juan County Community Development Department: https://www.sjcounty.net/community-development
- Phone: (505) 334-4313
- Requirements: Permits for most construction projects
- Addressing Application required for 911 geo-location
- Submit plans for review; inspections at multiple stages
- Certificate of Occupancy required before occupancy
