## Building Permits
- Issued by Village of Pecos under state oversight
- Requirements: Submit building plans to Village Hall for Mayor approval; obtain State of NM Building Permit; return copy to Village
- Mobile Home Permits: $20; require state verification
- Contact: (505) 757-6591 | Forms: https://villageofpecosnm.org/forms.html
