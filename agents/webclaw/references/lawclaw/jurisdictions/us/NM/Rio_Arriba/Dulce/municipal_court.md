# Dulce Courts
## Jicarilla Apache Tribal Court
- Dulce is a census-designated place (CDP) within the Jicarilla Apache Nation; no municipal court exists
- Jicarilla Apache Tribal Court handles civil, criminal, and family cases under tribal law
- Phone: (575) 759-3366
- Mailing Address: PO Box 128, Dulce, NM 87528
- Fax: (575) 759-3721
- Appeals: Southwest Intertribal Court of Appeals (SWITCA)
