## Building Permits
- Planning & Zoning Department: https://www.losranchosnm.gov/forms
- Phone: (505) 344-6582 | Contact: Michelle Austin — maustin@losranchosnm.gov
- Requirements: Submit two complete sets of plans; apply in person or via drop box
- Permits Required: Construction, additions, pools, fences, re-roofing, demolition, solar panels
- Inspections required; schedule by phone or email
- Certificate of Occupancy issued upon final approval
