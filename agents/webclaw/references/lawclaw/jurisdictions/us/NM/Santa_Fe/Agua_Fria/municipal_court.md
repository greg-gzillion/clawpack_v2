## Community Information
- Agua Fria is a census-designated place (CDP) and former village ~6 miles southwest of Santa Fe
- Governed by City of Santa Fe and Santa Fe County; no independent municipal government
- City of Santa Fe Website: https://www.santafenm.gov
- Santa Fe County Website: https://www.santafecountynm.gov
