# Pojoaque Courts
## Pueblo of Pojoaque Tribal Court
- Address: 2 Petroglyph Circle, Santa Fe, NM 87506
- Phone: (505) 455-2271
- Jurisdiction: Civil, criminal, family, and traffic matters under tribal law
- Website: https://pojoaque.org/community/tribal-court/
- Handles cases involving tribal members and those on tribal land

## Detention Center
- Pueblo of Pojoaque Department of Corrections — 78 Cities of Gold Road, Santa Fe, NM 87506
- Phone: (505) 455-2278 (via Police Dispatch)
- Capacity: Houses pretrial and sentenced individuals under tribal authority
- Inmate Information: Contact through Police Department
- Note: Short-term detention; long-term inmates may be transferred under intergovernmental agreements
## Building Permits
- Pueblo of Pojoaque Planning & Zoning Department for tribal lands
- Contact: Pueblo of Pojoaque Administration — (505) 455-4500
- Requirements: Submit plans for review; permits required for construction, remodeling, and electrical work
- Regulations: Follow tribal building codes and land use policies
- For non-tribal properties: Santa Fe County Building and Development Services
