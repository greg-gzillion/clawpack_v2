# Pojoaque — Local Law Resources

- Tribal Administration: 2 Petroglyph Circle, Santa Fe, NM 87506 — (505) 455-4500
- Governor: Jenelle Roybal | Government: Elected Tribal Council under 1934 Indian Reorganization Act
## Law Enforcement
- Pueblo of Pojoaque Police Department — https://pojoaque.org/community/tribal-police/
- Address: 2 Petroglyph Circle, Santa Fe, NM 87506
- Emergency: 911 | Non-Emergency: (505) 455-2278 | Records: (505) 455-0174
- Serves Pueblo lands and surrounding areas under tribal and federal jurisdiction
## Courts
- Pueblo of Pojoaque Tribal Court — 2 Petroglyph Circle, Santa Fe, NM 87506 — (505) 455-2271
- Jurisdiction: Civil, criminal, family, and traffic matters under tribal law
- Website: https://pojoaque.org/community/tribal-court/
- Santa Fe County District Court: 225 Montezuma Avenue, Santa Fe, NM 87501 — (505) 455-8200
  Website: https://www.nmcourts.gov
## Detention Center
- Pueblo of Pojoaque Department of Corrections — 78 Cities of Gold Road, Santa Fe, NM 87506
- Phone: (505) 455-2278 (via Police Dispatch)
- Houses pretrial and sentenced individuals under tribal authority
- Short-term detention; long-term inmates may be transferred under intergovernmental agreements
## Building Permits
- Pueblo of Pojoaque Planning & Zoning for tribal lands — (505) 455-4500
- Requirements: Submit plans for review; permits for construction, remodeling, electrical work
- Follow tribal building codes and land use policies
- For non-tribal properties: Santa Fe County Building and Development Services
