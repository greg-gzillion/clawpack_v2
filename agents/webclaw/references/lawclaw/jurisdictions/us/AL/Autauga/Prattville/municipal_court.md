## Building Permits
- Issued by: City of Prattville Building Department
- Address: 102 West Main Street, Prattville, AL 36067
- Phone: (334) 595-0400
- Permits required for all construction activities (Chapters 26 and 105, Code of Ordinances)
- Residential: Basic application and review
- Commercial: Site plan and code review required before permit issuance
- Stormwater grading permit required for commercial construction with grading
- Building permit null and void if work not begun within 6 months
- Online data: City of Prattville Building Permits GIS Hub
- Website: https://prattvilleal.gov/departments/planning-and-development/building-department/
