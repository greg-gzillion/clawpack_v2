# Edina Courts
## Court Information
- Edina does not have a municipal court
- Former Hennepin County Southdale Courthouse closed in 2019
- Cases now handled at Ridgedale Courthouse or downtown Minneapolis
- Court Website: https://www.mncourts.gov
