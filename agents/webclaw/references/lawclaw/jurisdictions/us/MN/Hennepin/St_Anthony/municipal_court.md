# St. Anthony Village Courts
## Court Information
- St. Anthony Village does not have a municipal court
- Legal matters handled by Hennepin County District Court (Hennepin County portion)
- Court Website: https://www.mncourts.gov/district/10
- Also served by Ramsey County District Court (Ramsey County portion)
- Ramsey Court Website: https://www.mncourts.gov/district/1
