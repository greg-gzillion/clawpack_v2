# Wagner City (Charles Mix County, SD) Courts
## Municipal Court / Tribal Court Jurisdiction
- Wagner does NOT have its own municipal court
- Cases handled by Charles Mix County courts OR Yankton Sioux Tribal Court
- Charles Mix County Court Services: https://ujs.sd.gov
- Yankton Sioux Tribal Court: https://www.yanktonsiouxtribe.net
- South Dakota Unified Judicial System: https://ujs.sd.gov
- Note: No municipal court; County or Tribal courts serve city
