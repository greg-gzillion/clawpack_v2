# Clarkstown Courts
## Clarkstown Justice Court
- Address: 20 Maple Avenue, New City, NY 10956
- Phone: (845) 639-5960
- Chief Court Clerk: Candyce Draper
- Hours: 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM
- Website: https://www.clarkstown.gov/justice-court
- Four Town Justices; one of the largest criminal case courts in NY state
- Handles: Criminal cases (misdemeanors up to 364 days jail), Vehicle & Traffic, Town Code violations, felony arraignments/bail/preliminary hearings, Landlord-Tenant cases, Small Claims
- Drug Court: Clarkstown-originated, now located in Rockland County
- Jurisdiction: Felony cases transferred to Rockland County Grand Jury after preliminary hearing
