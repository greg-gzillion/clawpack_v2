## Building Permits
- Code Enforcement Division (Building) — (914) 734-1010
- Director: John Schembari
- Email: codes@townofcortlandt.com
- Department Page: https://www.townofcortlandtny.gov/cn/webpage.cfm?tpid=2513
- Address: 1 Heady Street, Cortlandt Manor, NY 10567
- Permits Required: New construction, additions/alterations/renovations, roofing, siding (if sheathing replaced), decks, sheds over 100 sq ft, fences over 6 feet, HVAC, plumbing, electrical work
- No Permit Required: Sheds under 100 sq ft not permanently affixed, re-siding without sheathing replacement, patios on grade, solid fuel burning devices installed before Jan 1, 1984
- All work must comply with Town Zoning Code and NYS Uniform Code
