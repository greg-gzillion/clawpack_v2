## Building Permits
- Building & Planning Department — (518) 233-2127
- Address: 97 Mohawk Street, Cohoes, NY 12047
- Email: Code@ci.cohoes.ny.us
- Website: https://www.ci.cohoes.ny.us
- Permit Application: General Building Permit Application
- Fee: 1.3% of total project cost, minimum $50
- Processing Time: 5–10 business days
- Validity: 6 months from issuance
- Required Documents: Site plans showing property lines, structures, and utilities; proof of liability insurance ($1M minimum for contractors); workers' compensation and disability insurance certificates
- Permits Required For: Fences, decks, pools, siding, roof replacement, and major repairs
