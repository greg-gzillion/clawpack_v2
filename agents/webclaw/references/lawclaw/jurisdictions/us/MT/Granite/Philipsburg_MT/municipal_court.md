# Philipsburg Municipal Court
## Court Information
- Philipsburg does not have a standalone municipal court. Cases are handled by Granite County Justice Court.
- Address: Granite County Courthouse, 220 N Sansome Street, Philipsburg, MT 59858
- Phone: (406) 859-3771 (Clerk of Court)
- Hours: Contact clerk for details; typical county office hours Mon–Fri 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- Court Website: https://courts.mt.gov
