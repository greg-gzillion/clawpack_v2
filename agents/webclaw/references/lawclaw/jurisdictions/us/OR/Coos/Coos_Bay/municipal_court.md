## Building Permits
- Community Development – Building Codes Division — (541) 269-8918
- Address: 500 Central Avenue, Coos Bay, OR 97420
- Website: https://www.coosbayor.gov/services/documents-permits/building-permits-179
- PermitFinder Tool: https://www.coosbayor.gov/services/documents-permits/find-a-document
- ePermitting (State Portal): https://epermitting.oregon.gov
- When Permit Required: Structures >200 sq ft (residential) or >120 sq ft (commercial); roof height >15 ft; electrical, plumbing, mechanical, structural changes; sewer connections involving public right-of-way
- Application: Submit plans online or in person; pay plan review fees; permit issued after approval
- Inspections: Schedule during and after work
