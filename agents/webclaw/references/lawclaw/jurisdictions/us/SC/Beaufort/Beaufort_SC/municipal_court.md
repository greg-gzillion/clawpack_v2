# Beaufort City (Beaufort County, SC) Courts
## Beaufort Municipal Court
- Serves: Beaufort City
- Location: 1901 Boundary Street, Beaufort, SC 29902
- Phone: (843) 255-3200 (via City Hall)
- Sessions: Every Monday and Thursday at 3:00 PM
- Judges: Professional, impartial adjudication; jury trials scheduled quarterly
- Online Info: https://www.cityofbeaufort.org/218/Municipal-Court
- Jurisdiction: Misdemeanor offenses, violations of city and state laws within city limits
- South Carolina Judicial Branch: https://www.sccourts.org
- Note: Twice-weekly court sessions (Mon & Thu); quarterly jury trials
- Handles: Misdemeanor offenses, city/state law violations
