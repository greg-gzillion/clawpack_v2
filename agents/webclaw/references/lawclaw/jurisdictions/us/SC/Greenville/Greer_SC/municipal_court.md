## Building Permits
- Office: Planning & Development Department
- Address: 301 E Poinsett Street, Greer, SC 29651
- Phone: (864) 848-2175
- Permit Portal: eTRAKiT
- Website: https://www.cityofgreersc.gov/page/planning-and-development-permits
- Required Permits: New construction, additions, remodels, demolitions, decks, fences, pools, sheds, electrical, plumbing, HVAC
- Process: Submit plans (stamped for commercial) → Pay fees → Permit approval → Schedule inspections
- Exempt: Painting, gutters, small storage buildings (zoning approval may still apply)
