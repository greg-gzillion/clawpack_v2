# Belton City (Anderson County, SC) Courts
## Belton Municipal Division Court
- Serves: Belton City
- Jurisdiction: Violations issued by Belton Code Enforcement and Animal Control
- Circuit: Part of the 17th Judicial Circuit
- Court Sessions: Twice monthly on Tuesdays at 9:00 AM
- Dockets for Incarcerated Defendants: Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays at 1:00 PM
- South Carolina Judicial Branch: https://www.sccourts.org
- Note: Twice-monthly court; separate docket schedule for incarcerated defendants
- Handles: Traffic violations, city ordinance violations, code enforcement, animal control
