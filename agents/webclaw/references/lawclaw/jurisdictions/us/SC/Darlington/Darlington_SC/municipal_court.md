## Building Permits
- Office: Codes Enforcement Office
- Department Page: https://www.cityofdarlington.com/codes-enforcement/
- Phone: (843) 398-4029
- Address: 400 Pearl Street, Darlington, SC 29532
- Codes Enforcement Officer & Building Inspector: Sheila Jones — ext. 106
- Codes Enforcement Assistant Officer: Nita Howard — ext. 105
- Permit Requirement: Any work over $200 requires permits
- Required Permits: Building, structural, mechanical, plumbing, electrical (separate permits for each)
- Contractor Requirements: City business license + state license + submitted contract
- Inspections: Panel box labeling, hot water pan, exit signs, cover plates, fire extinguisher, fire alarms, no extension cords, 4" lettering for E-911
- Vacant Building Registration Program (since Jan 2022): Properties vacant >30 days must register
- Historic Landmarks Commission: Certificate of Appropriateness $65 for historic district properties
- Demolition List: Published online
- Permits Required: Building, structural, mechanical, plumbing, electrical, vacant building, historic review
