# St. James Courts

- St. James does not have a standalone municipal court
- Legal matters handled by Brunswick County District and Superior Courts
- Brunswick County Courthouse: 300 Government Center Dr NE, Bolivia, NC 28422
- Court Website: https://www.nccourts.gov/locations/brunswick
