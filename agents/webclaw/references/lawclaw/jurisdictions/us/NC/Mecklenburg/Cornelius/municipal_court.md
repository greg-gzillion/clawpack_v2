# Cornelius Courts
## Court Information
- Cornelius does not have its own municipal court
- Legal matters handled by Mecklenburg County District and Superior Courts
- District Court Website: https://www.nccourts.gov/courts/district-court/district-26
- Mecklenburg County Courthouse: 832 E 4th St, Charlotte, NC 28202

## Police Department
- Cornelius Police Department
- Address: 21445 Catawba Ave, Cornelius, NC 28031
- Emergency: 911
