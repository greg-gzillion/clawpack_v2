# Elizabeth City Courts
## Court Information
- Elizabeth City does not have a standalone municipal court
- Legal matters handled by Pasquotank County District Court
- Pasquotank County Courthouse: 206 E. Main Street, Elizabeth City, NC 27909
- Clerk of Court: (252) 331-4600
- Court Website: https://www.nccourts.gov/locations/pasquotank

## Detention Center
- Pasquotank County Detention Center — 527 Commerce Drive, Elizabeth City, NC 27906
- Phone: (252) 331-4881
- Capacity: ~200 inmates
- Official Website: https://www.pasquotankcountync.org/sheriff
- Inmate Search: Contact Sheriff's Office directly
- Note: Not to be confused with Pasquotank Correctional Institution (state prison)
