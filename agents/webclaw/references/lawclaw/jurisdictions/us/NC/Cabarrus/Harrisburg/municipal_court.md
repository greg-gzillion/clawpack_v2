# Harrisburg Courts
## Court Information
- Harrisburg does not have a standalone municipal court
- Legal matters handled by Cabarrus County District and Superior Courts
- Cabarrus County Courthouse: 215 N. Spring Street, Concord, NC 28025
- Court Website: https://www.nccourts.gov/locations/cabarrus
