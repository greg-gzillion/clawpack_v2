# Albemarle Courts
## Court Information
- Albemarle does not have a standalone municipal court
- Legal matters handled by Stanly County District and Superior Courts
- District Court Website: https://www.nccourts.gov/courts/district-court/district-24
- Stanly County Courthouse Address: 100 North First Street, Albemarle, NC 28001
- Phone: (704) 986-3600

- Phone: (704) 986-3734 (Main) | (704) 986-3714 (Inmate Info)
- Capacity: 133-135 inmates
- Official Website: https://www.stanlysheriff.us/detention-center/
- Inmate Search: Not available online; call for information

## Building Permits
- Administered by Stanly County Central Permitting Department (not city directly)
- Online Portal: OpenGov Permitting
- In Person: Stanly County Central Permitting, 1000 North First Street, Suite 13, Albemarle, NC 28001
- Phone: (704) 986-3667
- Process: City of Albemarle Coordination Form (via albemarlenc.gov) then county permit via OpenGov
