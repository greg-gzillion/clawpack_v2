## Building Permits
- Two-step process: Town Zoning Permit → Rockingham County Building Permit
- Town Zoning Permit:
  Address: 125-B Eastview St, Dayton, VA 22821
  Phone: (540) 879-2241
  Email: mroche@daytonva.us
  Fees: $25 minimum or $2.00 per 100 sq ft (whichever greater); $25 flat fee for signs/structures
  Processing Time: Allow at least 2 weeks
  Application: Download from town website
- Rockingham County Building Permit: Follows zoning approval
  Website: https://www.co.rockingham.va.us/building-inspections
