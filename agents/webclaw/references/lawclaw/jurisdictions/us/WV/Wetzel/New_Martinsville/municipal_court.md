## Building Permits
- Office: Building Department
- Address: 191 Main St, New Martinsville, WV 26155 (City Hall)
- Phone: (304) 455-9130
- Building Inspector: Joseph Hanna — (304) 455-9120
- Website: https://newmartinsville.com/departments/building-department/
- Permit Not Required: Maintenance work (painting, board replacement), interior jobs under $2,000 (no electrical/plumbing/structural), slab concrete
- Permit Required: County/State jobs up to $500 (no fee), asbestos removal, all electrical/plumbing/mechanical/structural
- Licenses Required: City License + State License for all contractors; Handyman License (jobs under $5,000); Contractors License (jobs over $5,000)
- Setbacks: 5 ft side, 10 ft between structures, 25 ft frontal
- Fees: No fee for County/State jobs up to $500; Residential $25 + $3/$1,000; Commercial $50 + $4/$1,000; Demolition $10
