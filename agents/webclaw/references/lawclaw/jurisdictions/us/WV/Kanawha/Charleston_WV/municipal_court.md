# Charleston City (Kanawha County, WV) Courts

## Charleston Municipal Court
- Serves: Charleston City
- Address: 501 Virginia St E, Charleston, WV 25301 (City Hall)
- Mailing Address: PO Box 2749, Charleston, WV 25301
- Phone: (304) 348-8079
- Fax: (304) 348-6894
- Email: municipal.court@cityofcharleston.org
- Judge: Matt Smith
- Court Sessions: Tuesdays and Wednesdays at 3:00 PM
- Courtroom Doors Open: 3:00 PM (enter via Court St side of City Hall; late entry denied — marked failure to appear, capias may issue, license may be suspended)
- Clerk's Office Hours: Mon–Fri 7:30 AM – 6:30 PM (Room 103, enter via Virginia St revolving doors)
- Online Payments: https://www.charlestonwvpayments.com (allow 10 business days for citation to appear)
- Payment Methods: Credit/debit card online; in person (certified check, cash, money order, personal check); mail (PO Box 2749); NO cash by mail; bond must be cash
- Court Docket: https://www.charlestonwv.gov/government/city-departments/municipal-court (online docket available)
- Continuances: Written request required 7 days in advance with good cause; doctor's excuse for illness; obituary/death certificate for funeral
- Parking: Shanklin Parking Building next to City Hall
- Security: Enter via CPD security scan
- Website: https://www.charlestonwv.gov/government/city-departments/municipal-court
- Note: HAS ACTIVE MUNICIPAL COURT
