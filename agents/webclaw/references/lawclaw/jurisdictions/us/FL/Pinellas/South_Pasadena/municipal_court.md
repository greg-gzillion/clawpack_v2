## Library
- South Pasadena does not have its own public library
- Residents receive subsidized library card for Pinellas County Library Cooperative (PCLC)
- Fee:  annually (City subsidizes  of the  PCLC fee)
- Process: Pay at City Hall, then redeem voucher at St. Pete Beach Library
- St. Pete Beach Library: 365 73rd Avenue, St. Pete Beach, FL 33706
- Library Info: https://www.mysouthpasadena.com/library
