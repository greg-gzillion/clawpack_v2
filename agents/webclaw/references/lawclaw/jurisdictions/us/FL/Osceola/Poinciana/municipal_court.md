# Poinciana CDP (Osceola/Polk Counties, FL) Courts

## Poinciana Municipal Court — NONE
- Status: **UNINCORPORATED — NO MUNICIPAL COURT**
- Poinciana is a census-designated place (CDP) spanning Osceola and Polk Counties, not an incorporated city
- Governed by both counties and the Association of Poinciana Villages (HOA)
- Legal matters handled by: **Osceola County Court System** or **Polk County Court System** depending on jurisdiction
- Osceola County Clerk of Court: https://www.osceolaclerk.com
- Polk County Clerk of Court: https://www.polkcountyclerk.net

## Community Information (Unincorporated)
- Poinciana is UNINCORPORATED — governed by Osceola County and Polk County
- Association of Poinciana Villages (HOA): https://www.apvcommunity.com
- Osceola County: https://www.osceola.org
- Polk County: https://www.polk-county.net
- Population: ~70,000
- Coordinates: 28.1429° N, 81.4730° W
