## Community Information (Unincorporated)
- Siesta Key is UNINCORPORATED — governed by Sarasota County
- County Website: https://scgov.net
- County Address: 1660 Ringling Blvd, Sarasota, FL 34236
- County Phone: (941) 861-5000 or 311
- Population: ~5,500
- Coordinates: 27.2978° N, 82.5361° W
- Note: Barrier island off Sarasota on the Gulf of Mexico; world-famous for Siesta Beach (consistently ranked #1 beach in USA by TripAdvisor/Dr. Beach) with 99% quartz sand; known for Siesta Key Village, Crescent Beach, and Turtle Beach; popular for sunset celebrations at the beach
