# Laramie City (Albany County, WY) Courts

## Laramie Municipal Court
- Address: 406 E Ivinson St, Laramie, WY 82070
- Phone: (307) 721-5205
- Email: municourt@cityoflaramie.org
- Hours: Mon-Thu 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM, Fri 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM
- Sessions: Mon, Tue, Thu at 9:00 AM (in person or Zoom)
- Jurisdiction: City of Laramie ordinances, adopted WY state statutes, Laramie PD and UW PD citations, traffic, DWUI
- NOT handled: Albany County Sheriff citations, WY Highway Patrol (go to Albany County Circuit Court)
- Zoom requests: email municourt@cityoflaramie.org with full name and DOB
- URL: https://www.cityoflaramie.org/95/Municipal-Court

## Albany County Circuit Court
- Handles: Albany County Sheriff citations, WY Highway Patrol citations, misdemeanors, civil cases
