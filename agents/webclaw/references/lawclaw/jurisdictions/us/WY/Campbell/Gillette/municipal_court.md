# Gillette City (Campbell County, WY) Courts

## Gillette Municipal Court
- Serves: Gillette City
- Address: 201 E 5th St, Gillette, WY 82716 (City Hall)
- Phone: (307) 686-5254
- Email: municipalcourt@gillettewy.gov
- Clerk of Court: Lindsey Zwirn
- Judges: Hon. Chris R. Ringer, Hon. Alison O. Gee
- Office Hours: Mon 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM, Tue 7:30 AM – 2:00 PM, Wed 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM, Thu 8:00 AM – 1:00 PM, Fri 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM (closed weekends & holidays)
- Arraignments: Tuesday mornings at 8:30 AM (check-in at Municipal Court window begins 7:30 AM, Council Chambers, City Hall)
- Trials: Thursday mornings at 8:30 AM (Council Chambers, City Hall)
- Subpoenas: Must be requested no later than 2 weeks prior to trial date
- Jurisdiction: All criminal violations of City of Gillette ordinances; citations issued by Gillette Police Department ONLY; fines do not exceed $750; imprisonment does not exceed 6 months for any one offense
- Bench Warrants/OTSC: Issued for failure to appear, failure to pay fines, or failure to comply with court orders; warrant list displayed on City website and GPA Channel 192
- Online Payments: Available at gillettewy.gov or call (307) 686-5254 and leave voicemail
- After-Hours Payments: Online, phone voicemail, or counter during office hours
- Filings: Email to municipalcourt@gillettewy.gov, mail, or drop off at counter
- Under 18: Must bring parent or legal guardian
- Court Etiquette: No hats, cell phones off, no food/drink, no weapons (except LEO)
- URL: https://www.gillettewy.gov/Departments/Public-Affairs/Municipal-Court
- Note: HAS ACTIVE MUNICIPAL COURT
