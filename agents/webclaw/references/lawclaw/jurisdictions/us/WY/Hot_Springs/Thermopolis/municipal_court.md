## Town Website
- https://thermopoliswy.gov/
- Town Hall: 417 Arapahoe St, Thermopolis, WY 82443
- Government Type: Mayor–Council
- COUNTY SEAT of Hot Springs County (least populous county in Wyoming with a county seat over 500)
- Population: ~2,800
- Coordinates: 43.6475° N, 108.1995° W
- Note: On the Bighorn River at the mouth of Wind River Canyon; home to the **World's Largest Mineral Hot Springs** (Hot Springs State Park); known for the Wyoming Dinosaur Center, Star Plunge, Thermopolis Teepee Pools, and nearby Boysen Reservoir; named from Greek "thermo" (hot) + "polis" (city)
