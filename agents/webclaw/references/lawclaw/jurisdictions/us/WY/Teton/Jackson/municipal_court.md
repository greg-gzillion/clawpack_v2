## Building Permits
- Office: Planning & Building Department
- Address: 150 E Pearl Ave, Jackson, WY 83001 (Town Hall)
- Phone: (307) 733-3079
- Website: https://www.jacksonwy.gov/181/Building
- IMPORTANT — NO over-the-counter permitting. ALL permits are electronic only.
- Commercial & residential permits: Submit via email to Town Building (200 MB max per document; large files must be split into multiple PDFs). Submittals after 3:00 PM considered received next business day.
- Mechanical, electrical, plumbing permits: Submitted via SmartGov portal (requires Town of Jackson Contractor license).
- Trade permits: MUST be obtained prior to starting work, or penalty fee assessed. 48-hour processing time.
- Payment: Via SmartGov portal, check at front desk, or credit card (phone/in person). 2.55% processing fee for credit card payments.
- Inspection scheduling: NOT available in SmartGov portal; refer to building inspection tab on website.
- Nonrefundable plan review fee required for all commercial submittals.
- NO hard/paper copies accepted.
