# Holmen Village (La Crosse County, WI) Courts
## Joint Municipal Court (serves Holmen)
- Serves: Holmen Village, Onalaska, West Salem, Bangor, Campbell, Shelby, Rockland
- Court Location: Onalaska City Hall, 415 Main St, Onalaska, WI 54650
- Judge: Elected at large for four-year term
- Clerk: Hired by the City of Onalaska
- Website: https://www.holmenwi.gov/ordinance/chapter-108-municipal-court-joint/
- Note: JOINT MUNICIPAL COURT — Holmen participates in a joint municipal court with multiple La Crosse County communities; court sessions held at Onalaska City Hall
