## Building Permits
- City of Delphos Zoning and Safety Services Department — (419) 695-4010
- Address: 608 N Canal St, Delphos, OH 45833
- Zoning Inspector: Safety Services Director
- Website: https://www.cityofdelphos.com/zoning
- Zoning Map (PDF): Available through city website
- Codified Ordinances – Chapter 1135 (Permits and Fees): https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/delphos/latest/delphos_oh/0-0-0-1
- Permits Required: Construction, additions, pools (48" or higher), sheds, fences, driveways, HVAC, electrical, plumbing
- Application: Zoning permit required before building permit; submit application via form prescribed by Zoning Inspector
- Note: Applications must be submitted on forms from Zoning Inspector; no direct PDF downloads available online
- Inspections: Required at multiple stages
- Certificate of Occupancy: Required before occupancy
