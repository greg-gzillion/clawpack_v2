## Building Permits
- Community Development / Building Services — (330) 678-8107 (Building), (330) 678-8464 (Code Enforcement)
- Address: 930 Overholt Road, Kent, OH 44240
- Website: https://www.kentohio.gov/business-building-development/permits-and-inspections/
- Portal: Customer Self-Service Portal
- Permits Required: New construction, additions, decks, roofing (structural), plumbing, electrical, HVAC, fences (over 6 ft), pools
- Fees: $25 base + $0.05/sq ft (building), $25 flat (zoning), $50 (decks)
- Inspections: Required at foundation, framing, electrical, plumbing, final
