# Elk Township Courts
## Municipal Court
- Elk Township does not have its own municipal court
- Served by Gloucester County Central Municipal Court
- Address: 1 N. Broad Street, Woodbury, NJ 08096
- Phone: (856) 384-5500
- Court Website: https://www.njcourts.gov/courts/municipal/court-directory.html
- Payments: www.njmcdirect.com
- Handles: Traffic violations, disorderly persons offenses, municipal ordinances
