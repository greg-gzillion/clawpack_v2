## Building Permits
- Building Department: https://www.waldwicknj.org/departments/building
- Construction Official: Joseph G. Mysliwiec (ext. 233) | Zoning Official: Joseph G. Mysliwiec
- Technical Assistant: Nancy Koch (ext. 232)
- Requirements: Permits for construction, additions, pools, fences, HVAC, electrical, plumbing

- Site plan approval for commercial/multi-unit; Certificate of Occupancy required
