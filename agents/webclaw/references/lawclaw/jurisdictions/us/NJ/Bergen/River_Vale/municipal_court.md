## Building Permits
- River Vale Construction and Zoning Department
- Reference: eCode360 Land Use Chapter
- Permit Required For: Construction, additions, changes of use, site plans, driveways, parking lots (4+ vehicles)
- Site Plan Approval: Required for commercial, industrial, and non-residential projects
- Exemptions: Single-family homes, accessory structures (garages, pools), minor interior repairs
- Change of Use: Requires approval from Zoning Official and Township Engineer
- Forms & Code: eCode360 Land Use Chapter
