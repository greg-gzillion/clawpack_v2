# Allamuchy Township Courts
## Municipal Court
- No Local Court: Allamuchy residents use Warren County Joint Municipal Court
- Online Payments: www.njmcdirect.com
- Handles: Traffic violations, disorderly persons offenses, municipal ordinances

- Allamuchy Township does not operate its own police department
- Law enforcement provided by New Jersey State Police (Hackettstown Barracks)
- Emergency: 911
- Non-Emergency (NJSP): (908) 852-3130
