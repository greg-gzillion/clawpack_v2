# Spotswood Courts
## Municipal Court
- Spotswood does not have its own municipal court
- Served by Middlesex County Central Municipal Court
- Address: 100 Bayard Street, New Brunswick, NJ 08901
- Phone: (732) 249-3500
- Court Website: https://www.njcourts.gov/courts/municipal/court-directory.html
- Payments: www.njmcdirect.com
- Handles: Traffic violations, disorderly persons offenses, municipal ordinances
