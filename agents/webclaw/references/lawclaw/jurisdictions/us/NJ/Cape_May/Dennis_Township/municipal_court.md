# Dennis Township Courts
## Municipal Court
- Dennis Township does not have its own municipal court
- Served by Cape May County Central Municipal Court
- Address: 678 N. Delsea Drive, Cape May Court House, NJ 08210
- Phone: (609) 465-1211
- Court Website: https://www.njcourts.gov/courts/municipal/court-directory.html
- Payments: www.njmcdirect.com
- Handles: Traffic violations, disorderly persons offenses, municipal ordinances
