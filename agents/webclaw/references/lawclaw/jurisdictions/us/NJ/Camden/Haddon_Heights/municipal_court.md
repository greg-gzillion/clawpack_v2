## Building Permits
- Haddon Heights Construction and Zoning Department
- Website: https://www.haddonhts.com/construction/page/permitting
- Permits Required For: Construction, additions, sheds, pools, fences, HVAC, electrical, plumbing
- Minimum Fees: Sheds (100–200 sq ft): $75; Sheds (201+ sq ft): $200; Open decks (101–200 sq ft): $175
- Inspections: Required; schedule via phone
- Certificate of Occupancy: Required before property sale
